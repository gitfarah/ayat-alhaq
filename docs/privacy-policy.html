Privacy Policy — Ayat al-Haq (آيات الحق)

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Ayat al-Haq is a Quran reading, listening and prayer-times app. We respect your privacy. This app has no user accounts, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share your personal data with anyone.

Information the app uses

  • Location (optional). If — and only if — you tap “Use my current location” for prayer times, the app reads your device’s location while you are using the app. Your coordinates are used to look up accurate prayer times and your city name. They are stored only on your device (to avoid re-asking) and are never uploaded to any server owned by us. You can use a manually chosen city instead and never grant location access at all.
  • On-device data. Your bookmarks, highlights, khatma progress, chosen reciter, language and appearance settings, and any Quran pages / recitation audio / tafsir you download are saved only on your device. They are never transmitted to us and are removed when you delete the app.

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifier.

Third-party services the app contacts

To provide its features the app requests data over standard secure (HTTPS) connections from the following public services. Only the technical request needed for the feature is sent (e.g. a city name or coordinates for prayer times); no personal profile is shared.

  • AlAdhan (aladhan.com) — daily prayer times.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (openstreetmap.org) — turns GPS coordinates into a city name for display.
  • AlQuran Cloud (alquran.cloud) — optional verse translations.
  • Islamic Network CDN (islamic.network) — recitation audio.
  • Quranpedia (github.com/quranpedia) — Mushaf page images.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing any data it receives.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
